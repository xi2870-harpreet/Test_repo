## Question
How can a customer track Labs 2.0 usage?

## Answer (as of 2026-11-12)
Use **Reporting** in the left menu, which covers tracks and labs. Select the Labs filter and a date range:
- **Activity:** plays, unique users, average time spent and completion rate, with a breakdown per lab.
- **Sessions:** each lab session with progress, time spent and how it ended.
- **Users:** activity per learner.

Any of these can be exported to CSV. The Tracks filter allows a comparison with tracks.

## Known gaps at that date
- The **Usage page** (sandbox hours and Hot Starts) covered tracks only; Labs 2.0 was not included.
- **Hot Starts** were not available for labs.
