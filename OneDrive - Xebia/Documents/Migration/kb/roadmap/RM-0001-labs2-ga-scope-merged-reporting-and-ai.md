## Question
What is and is not in Labs 2.0 at GA?

## Answer (as recorded before GA)
- **Merged reporting:** not available at GA. The focus was polishing and removing bugs; it was planned to release shortly after GA.
- **AI:** the GA release is the assistant first, not the full plan and research flows.
- **Custom HTML and CSS in content:** not supported at GA (see KI-0003).
