## Question
Can a customer reach VMs created in a lab from outside Instruqt, for example with an external VNC client or shell?

## Answer (as of 2026-11-12)
Not supported at the time. A Support reply said it was on the roadmap and expected "in the next two months" (that is, around January 2027), and that the customer would be told on release. No release was recorded in the ticket.

## Notes
One ticket, one person's statement, so Unverified. Check Linear and the public docs for whether it has shipped, and never give the customer a date from this entry. For Tracks, external access on a VM is a separate setting (see PR-0005).
